A week is 168 hours. Each one is a square.

Instead of planning, colour an hour in once it has passed. By Sunday night the board is a picture of where your week actually went. No account, no server, no ads — free, with every feature included. For iPhone, iOS 17 and later. Coming soon to the App Store.

The boardEvery hour of the week on one screen. Tap a purpose, or drag to paint.
Days and hoursNotes, voice notes, side tasks, habits, weekly hour sounds and rules.
Targets and rhythm"At least 56 h of sleep", "at most 14 h of scrolling"; your hours' rhythm over 8 weeks.
WidgetsToday or the whole week on the Home Screen, unknown hours on the Lock Screen.
With JustDumpTimed tasks you tell JustDump land in their hours in Kare Kare.
